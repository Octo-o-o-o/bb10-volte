Download and install a BAR

For rooted phones with an existing compatible BAR installer.

Use an existing BAR installer

For Q10 SQN100-3, Q20 SQC100-2 and Passport SQW100-4 on OS 10.3.3.3216; the new integrated package has not been retested on Q10. Root alone does not provide an installation entry point. Check your BARinstaller works with your Root method.

Download BAR · 0.1.0.19SHA-256

  1. Wait for the system Root success notification.
  2. Download the BAR above, verify its complete same-release SHA-256 and close the existing app before upgrading.
  3. Open it through your compatible BAR installer, accept normal prompts and check the installed version is 0.1.0.19.
  4. Open the app and follow the mobile guide.

This is the same BAR bundled with the desktop app. Phone-only first installation has not been separately accepted; use the desktop installer if your local entry is unavailable.

Advanced: AutoRoot v4 terminal

Requires a working local Root terminal and the verified v4 installer. Download the same-version bootstrap ZIP, verify its checksum and follow its INSTALL.txt. v2/v3 are not interchangeable.

Bootstrap ZIP · SHA-256
